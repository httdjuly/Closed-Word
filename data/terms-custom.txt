# Domain terms the embedding pack does not have, and the everyday words that mean
# the same thing.
#
# `deno task pack:terms --write` reads this file, builds a vector for each term by
# averaging the vectors of its expansion, and appends it to data/vectors.bin. That
# makes the term guessable *and* rankable — before this, typing "rma" got you
# "not in the word list", because guesses are checked against the 50,000-word
# lexicon rather than against a word list we choose.
#
# Format:  term = word word word
#
# Rules for a good expansion:
#   - only words already in the pack (the script tells you which are missing);
#   - the words somebody would use to explain the term out loud, in the order
#     they would say them;
#   - three to six of them. One word makes the term a synonym of that word and
#     nothing else; a dozen averages the meaning away into mush.
#
# The result is a *composed* vector, not a learned one. It puts the term next to
# the right neighbourhood — rma lands beside return, warranty and repair — which
# is all a semantic guessing game needs. It is not a claim about how the term is
# used in real text.
